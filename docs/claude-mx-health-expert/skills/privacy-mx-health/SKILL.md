# Privacy for Private Healthcare in Mexico

## Baseline
Treat health data as sensitive personal data. Apply the current LFPDPPP and applicable implementing instruments to private clinics.

Load `references/privacy-controls.md`.

## Required reasoning
Separate:
- clinical consent
- personal-data consent/authorization
- secondary/commercial purposes
- external transfer/disclosure

Do not use one generic checkbox as evidence for all four.

## ARCO workflow
Model request, identity verification, representation, request type, timestamps, deadline, resolution, evidence and legal-retention conflict.

## Third parties
Determine whether each third party is processor/encargado, recipient, independent controller/responsible, or another role under the applicable framework. Do not label automatically.

## Breaches
Capture discovery date, event date, scope, categories affected, sensitive data, containment, analysis, remediation, required notifications and evidence.

## Current-law rule
Because Mexican privacy law changed materially in 2025, never rely on pre-2025 summaries without validating against current official text.
